QUESTION 1 - INNER JOIN
=========================================================

SCENARIO:
The hospital administration wants a report showing
each department and its department head.

QUERY:

SELECT d.name AS Department,
       p.name AS Head_Physician
FROM department d
JOIN physician p
ON d.head = p.employeeid;

OUTPUT:

Department          Head_Physician
----------------------------------
General Medicine    Percival Cox
Surgery             John Wen
Psychiatry          Molly Clock

The HR manager wants to know how many physicians

QUESTION 7 - GROUP BY + HAVING
=========================================================

SCENARIO:
The HR team wants to find positions occupied
by more than one physician.

QUERY:

SELECT position,
       COUNT(*) AS Total_Employees
FROM physician
GROUP BY position
HAVING COUNT(*) > 1;

------------------------------------------------------
Surgical Attending Physician                 3

FROM department d
